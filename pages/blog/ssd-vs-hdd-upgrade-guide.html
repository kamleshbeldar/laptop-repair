SSD vs HDD: The Definitive Answer

If your laptop is slow, the single biggest upgrade you can make is replacing the Hard Disk Drive (HDD) with a Solid State Drive (SSD). Here's everything you need to know.

Speed Comparison

  • Boot time: HDD: 60-120 seconds vs SSD: 8-15 seconds
  • App loading: HDD: 5-15 seconds vs SSD: 1-3 seconds
  • File copy speed: HDD: 80-120 MB/s vs SSD: 500-3500 MB/s
  • Durability: HDD: Moving parts (shock-sensitive) vs SSD: No moving parts

Types of SSDs Available

SATA SSD

Fits in the same bay as your existing HDD. 5x faster than HDD. Price: ₹2,000–₹6,000 for 256GB–1TB. Best for budget upgrades.

NVMe M.2 SSD

25x faster than HDD. Requires M.2 slot on motherboard (most laptops made after 2016 have one). Price: ₹3,000–₹9,000 for 256GB–1TB.
