Data Recovery in Lucknow – What You Need to Know

Losing important data from a laptop is stressful. At NexCore, we handle 50+ data recovery cases per month in Lucknow. Here's an honest guide to costs and what's recoverable.

Types of Data Loss & Recovery Cost

  • Accidental deletion / formatted drive: ₹2,000–₹4,000. High success rate (90%+).
  • OS crash / corrupted file system: ₹2,000–₹5,000. Good recovery possible.
  • Dead hard drive (clicking/not spinning): ₹4,000–₹12,000. Requires specialized tools.
  • SSD failure: ₹5,000–₹15,000. Complex, success depends on failure type.
  • Ransomware encrypted data: Case by case. Some decryptable, some not.

Our No Data, No Fee Policy

At NexCore, if we cannot recover your data, you pay nothing. We do a free assessment first to tell you recovery probability before starting.

What to Do When Drive Fails

  • Stop using the drive immediately — every write can destroy recoverable data
  • Do NOT run CHKDSK on a failing drive
  • Do NOT try DIY software recovery on a clicking drive
  • Bring the laptop / drive to us ASAP
Prevention is better than cure: Set up automatic cloud backup (Google Drive, OneDrive) for important files. It costs nothing and saves everything.
