MacBook Got Wet? Act Fast — First 30 Minutes Matter Most

Liquid spilled on a MacBook is a crisis — but fast action can save it. Here's exactly what to do, from someone who repairs MacBooks daily in Lucknow.

Immediate Steps (Do This NOW)

  • Immediately shut down — hold power button. Do NOT let it restart.
  • Unplug from power / charger
  • Flip upside down in tent position to drain
  • Remove any USB / Thunderbolt devices
  • Do NOT use a hairdryer (heat damages components)
  • Do NOT put in rice (urban myth — ineffective and wastes time)

What Happens Inside During Liquid Damage

When liquid contacts the MacBook logic board, it creates conductive bridges between circuits, causing shorts. If the laptop is powered on, these shorts can permanently damage chips. The faster you power off, the better.
