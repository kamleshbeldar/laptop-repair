Why Is Your Laptop Overheating?

Laptop overheating is one of the most common problems we see at our Lucknow service center. It causes random shutdowns, slow performance, and can permanently damage your motherboard if ignored.

Top 5 Causes of Laptop Overheating

  • Clogged cooling vents: Dust accumulation in fan blades and heatsink fins blocks airflow. Most common after 1-2 years.
  • Dried thermal paste: The paste between CPU/GPU and heatsink dries out after 3-4 years, reducing heat transfer efficiency.
  • Faulty fan: A fan that spins slowly or not at all causes rapid overheating.
  • Using on soft surfaces: Using laptop on bed or carpet blocks bottom vents.
  • Background processes: Malware or unwanted software running at 100% CPU usage.

DIY Fixes You Can Try

  • Use a hard, flat surface (desk, not bed)
  • Use a cooling pad with fans
  • Clean external vents with compressed air
  • Check Task Manager for high CPU processes
  • Update drivers (GPU, chipset)

When to Bring It to a Technician

If your laptop is still overheating after the above steps, it's time for professional service:

  • Internal dust cleaning (requires disassembly)
  • Thermal paste replacement (CPU + GPU)
  • Fan replacement if faulty
  • Heatsink re-seating
Lucknow summers are brutal. Laptop temperatures already run higher in hot weather. Get your laptop serviced before summer for best performance.
