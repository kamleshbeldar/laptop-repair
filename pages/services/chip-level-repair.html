What is Chip Level Repair?

Chip level repair involves diagnosing and replacing individual integrated circuits (ICs), transistors, MOSFETs, capacitors, and other components directly on the motherboard using professional BGA stations and microscopes.

Chip Level Services We Offer

  • BGA re-balling and rebonding (GPU, CPU, bridges)
  • IC replacement (charging IC, audio IC, power IC)
  • MOSFET / transistor replacement
  • Capacitor / inductor repair
  • EC (Embedded Controller) chip repair
  • BIOS chip re-programming
  • GPU re-flow and replacement
  • PCH / Bridge chip repair
